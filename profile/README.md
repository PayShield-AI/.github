## Mission

**PayShield-AI** is an open-source initiative dedicated to protecting consumers and merchants from sophisticated digital payment scams. By fusing real-time heuristic threat detection, behavioral NLP engines, and statistical benchmark models with developer payment APIs, we build defensive infrastructure that stops fraudulent transactions before funds move.

---

## Flagship Project

### [PayShield](https://github.com/PayShield-AI/PayShield)
*AI Payment Safety Assistant for the PayPal Developer Platform (PayPal AI Hackathon 2026).*

Online shopping scams often rely on urgent countdown timers, fake clearance domains, and tricks that convince buyers to bypass buyer protection (such as demands for *PayPal Friends & Family* or wire transfers). 

**PayShield** inspects product listings, pricing anomalies, and seller communications before money changes hands. It calculates a plain-words risk rating (**Low**, **Medium**, or **High**) and securely gates transaction execution via the **PayPal Orders API v2**.

- **Repository:** [github.com/PayShield-AI/PayShield](https://github.com/PayShield-AI/PayShield)
- **Demo Video:** [Watch Walkthrough on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=060fg4GyTac)
- **YouTube Channel:** [@Pay-Shield](https://www.youtube.com/@Pay-Shield)

### Core Guarantees
- **PayPal handles all payments securely:** All transactions execute exclusively through official PayPal Sandbox v2 APIs with Buyer Purchase Protection.
- **Zero cardholder data handled:** PayShield never touches, processes, or stores payment card numbers or sensitive banking credentials.
- **Consumer autonomy:** Safe purchases unlock instant checkout; elevated-risk purchases engage safety locks and require informed user confirmation.

---

## Core Focus Areas

- **Pre-Payment Threat Intelligence:** Evaluating domain authenticity, typosquatting, IDNA homograph attacks, and suspicious TLD reputations prior to checkout.
- **Economic Anomaly Modeling:** Comparing offered item prices against retail market benchmarks to detect high-probability non-delivery scams.
- **Agentic Commerce Security:** Natural language processing algorithms that identify coercive pressure tactics and off-platform payment diversion attempts.
- **Safe Checkout Gating:** Programmatic policy enforcement that locks checkout on fraudulent signals and requires conscious overrides.
